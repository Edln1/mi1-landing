FAQs

What exactly is OpenNote?

OpenNote is an AI-powered note-taking device built into open-ear earbuds.

It records conversations, transcribes them into text, and turns them into structured summaries, insights, and action items — automatically.

How is OpenNote different from a normal voice recorder?

Unlike traditional recorders, OpenNote doesn’t just capture audio.

It understands conversations — turning them into searchable transcripts, summaries, and answers you can actually use.

What can OpenNote record?

OpenNote can capture:

  • Phone and internet calls
  • In-person meetings
  • Podcasts, videos, and online content
  • Personal voice notes and ideas
Can I record without my phone?

Yes.
You can start recording directly from the OpenNote earbuds — no phone needed.

Is it legal to record conversations?

Recording laws vary by country and region.

OpenNote provides customizable recording notifications to help you stay compliant with local regulations.

We recommend always informing participants before recording.

What kind of summaries can OpenNote generate?

OpenNote automatically creates:

  • Key points
  • Action items
  • Structured summaries
  • Mind maps

You can also customize the format using templates or your own prompts.

Can I edit or refine the AI results?

Yes.
You can regenerate, edit, or ask AI to adjust the summary based on your needs — shorter, more detailed, or focused on specific topics.

Can I upload my own files?

Yes.
You can upload audio recordings, documents, or notes, and viaim App will process them with the same AI capabilities.

Do I need the app to use OpenNote?

The app is used to control recordings and manage device settings.

For deeper work like editing summaries and organizing notes, you can use the web platform.

Do I need a subscription to use OpenNote?

No.
You get 600 minutes of transcription every month for free — no subscription required.

Are there any hidden fees?

No.
All pricing is transparent, and you only upgrade if you choose to.

Is it just for work?

Not at all.
While OpenNote is built for professional use, it’s also perfect for learning, personal notes, and everyday ideas.

What’s the difference between OpenNote and RecDot?

Both share the same AI engine — 78-language transcription, summaries, and action items.

OpenNote uses an open-ear design with ENC and a 53-hour battery, so you stay aware of your surroundings while recording.
RecDot uses an in-ear design with 48dB ANC and a 36-hour battery, built for deeper noise isolation and one-tap recording from both the earbuds and the charging case.

Which one should I choose for meetings — OpenNote or RecDot?

If you want to stay aware of your surroundings — in-person meetings, walking, multitasking — while still capturing every word, OpenNote’s open-ear design is the better fit.

If you need noise isolation in loud environments or want to start recording from the charging case, RecDot is the better choice. Transcription and summary quality are identical on both.

Can OpenNote also start recording from the charging case like RecDot?

Currently, OpenNote’s FlashRecord starts recording directly from the earbuds.
Case-button recording is a RecDot-exclusive feature — see the RecDot product page for details.
